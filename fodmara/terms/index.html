What Fodmara is

Fodmara is a personal IBS and low-FODMAP diary: it stores what you log and shows counts, averages and shares of it. The food list shows which FODMAP groups foods typically contain, from public sources. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, does not use official FODMAP measurements and does not tell you what to eat. Talk to your doctor or dietitian before changing your diet.
