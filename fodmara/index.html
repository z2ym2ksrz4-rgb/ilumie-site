When to see a doctor

Blood in your stool, weight loss you can't explain, symptoms that wake you at night or a lasting change in bowel habits after 50 are not typical of IBS. Fodmara shows these signs clearly – a diary is never a reason to wait.

Free and Pro

Daily check-ins, meals, toilet visits, flare-ups, the calendar and the widget are free, forever. Fodmara Pro adds the reintroduction planner, patterns, the PDF report, PIN lock, encrypted backup and unlimited history – yearly with a 7-day free trial, or one lifetime purchase.

Fodmara is a self-observation diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, does not use official FODMAP measurements and gives no diet advice. Talk to your doctor or dietitian about what you log.
