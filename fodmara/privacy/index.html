Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Fodmara is an IBS and low-FODMAP diary. What you eat and how your gut feels is about as private as data gets, so the app is built so that we never see it.

Who we are

Fodmara is published by Therkildsen Group, CVR 46203798, Teglkrogen 10, 8800 Viborg, Denmark, under the Ilumie brand. Contact: fodmara@ilumie.com.

Where your data is

Everything you log – check-ins, meals, toilet visits, flare-ups, reintroduction tests, notes and medication names – is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no server-side copy. We cannot read, restore or delete your diary.

What we collect

Nothing. Fodmara contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting that identifies you.

Purchases

If you buy Fodmara Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. To check that a purchase is valid, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user id and the purchase receipt – never your diary.

Meal photos

A photo you attach to a meal is stored as a file inside the app on your device. It is not uploaded, not included in the encrypted backup and not in the PDF. Deleting the meal deletes the photo.

Apple Health (optional, Pro)

If you turn on Apple Health export, the app writes your flare-ups and toilet visits to Health as symptom entries (abdominal cramps, bloating, diarrhoea, constipation, nausea, heartburn). It never reads anything from Health. You can turn it off at any time; entries already written stay in Health until you delete them there.

Backup and PDF

An encrypted backup file or a PDF report is only created when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes. The backup is encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

Data deletion

Fodmara has no accounts and no server-side data, so you delete it yourself:

  • Delete in the app: Settings → Your data → Delete all data.
  • Delete the app: removing it from your phone deletes everything it stored.
  • Backup files you exported are yours; delete them where you saved them.

Not a medical device

Fodmara is a self-observation diary. It does not diagnose, does not use official FODMAP measurements and does not recommend a diet or treatment.

Children

Fodmara is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them (it collects none from anyone).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app's store page links to the new version.
