Support

Write to fodmara@ilumie.com – or use Settings → About → Send feedback in the app, which adds your app version for us.

What does the 0–10 number mean?

Your gut symptoms for the day as a whole, on your own scale – 0 is no symptoms, 10 is as bad as it gets. Compare it with your own days, not with other people.

Why are there no traffic lights or portion sizes?

How much of a food you tolerate is personal and depends a lot on the amount. Fodmara only shows which FODMAP groups a food typically contains; your dietitian decides what and how much.

How does a reintroduction test work?

Pick a group and a test food. On three days you eat a small, medium and large portion, then three pause days follow. Fodmara reminds you, shows your gut score next to the week before, and you record the result.

I changed phone – where is my diary?

Fodmara has no account, so data does not move by itself. Export an encrypted backup (Settings → Your data) on the old phone and import it on the new one.

Reminders don't show

Check that notifications are allowed for Fodmara in your phone's settings and that the daily reminder is turned on in Fodmara's settings.
