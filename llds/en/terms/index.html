Terms of use

Last updated 22 November 2026

Account

The app is for members of LL Dance Studio, their parents and trainers. The dance school approves all profiles. Your own account requires you to be 13 or older; younger dancers are under a parent's profile.

Good conduct

The team space is a safe place. Be kind and respectful – no bullying, no sharing of others' photos outside the app. Trainers and the administrator read along, and anything can be reported with one tap. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and repeated violations may lead to the account being closed.

Photos and video

Photos and choreography videos belong to LL Dance Studio and may only be used for your own practice – not shared on social media without agreement.

Purchases and membership

Prices are in Danish kroner incl. VAT. Shop and punch cards are paid via MobilePay; the order is confirmed once LL Dance Studio has registered the payment. Punch cards are valid 12 months from activation. Season price, instalments, trial lessons and right of withdrawal: see Membership.

Operations

The app is provided as is; we aim for high uptime but do not guarantee it. Ilumie may update the app and these terms – significant changes are announced in the app.
