Mini Angels – our strongest Mini Kids team

Mini Angels is an advanced+ team for the most committed and talented Mini Kids dancers who want to be challenged technically and creatively. On the team we work on: Stronger technique and body control, refining jumps, turns and movement. Expression and performance, so dancers learn to stand strong on stage. Community and joy, where the team cheers each other on and grows together. Competition preparation with choreography and targeted training for shows and competitions. Mini Angels is for dancers who want a little more and want to grow and have fun together.

Note: Audition

Admission to Mini Angels is by audition. Time and place are on the front page of the website.

Technique training

Thursday 15:45–16:30 with trainer Christine. An extra technique class that sharpens technical skills, works on advanced jumps, turns and tricks, and strengthens the small muscles. The aim is to lift performance and reduce injury risk. The technique class is included in the price.

Remember: two free trial lessons, always

You can always get two free trial lessons if you are unsure whether the style or level suits you. You can join a team at any point in the season, even though the season starts in August. Does not apply to elite teams.

Parent days

On beginner and advanced teams (ages 2–18) the first lesson of every month is a parent day. You may film and photograph your own child without flash. Spectators must be quiet, refrain from talking and set their phone to silent. On elite teams for kids and juniors, parents may only watch the last 5 minutes, once a month.
