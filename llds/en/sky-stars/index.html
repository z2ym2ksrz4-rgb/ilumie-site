Welcome to Sky Stars

Sky Stars is our advanced+ kids' showdance team for dancers who want to work seriously on their development. The level is high from the start, and you must be ready to be challenged both technically and in performance. The team also suits those who need a break from the elite teams but still want to maintain their level.

Sky Stars – for kids who want more than just dancing

When you join Sky Stars, you choose dance. You prioritise training, show up with motivation and drive, and are ready to go full out every time. Ready to give everything? Then this is where you belong.

Good to know

Within the first 4 lessons the trainer assesses whether the level suits each dancer. If it isn't the right match, you'll be told and the payment refunded so you can sign up for another team. By signing up you accept that this assessment may take place. By signing up or attending the first training you accept that you may be moved to Satellite Stars if the trainer judges that to be the right level. The sign-up price is therefore the same as for Satellite Stars. After the 4 trial lessons, those who continue on Sky Stars pay the remaining DKK 975.

On Sky Stars

We train at a high level with a focus on technique, expression and performance. Participation in competitions and extra training is expected. The calendar of training and competitions is further down the page. There is room for hard work and a strong community. We lift each other and grow together.

Technique training

Monday 17:20–18:20 with trainer Karoline Hoe. Technical skills are polished, working on advanced jumps, turns, tricks and strength exercises for the small muscles to reduce injury risk. Sky Stars and Satellite Stars train together. The technique class is included in the price.

Remember: two free trial lessons, always

You can always get two free trial lessons if you are unsure whether the style or level suits you. You can join a team at any point in the season, even though the season starts in August. Does not apply to elite teams.

Parent days

On beginner and advanced teams (ages 2–18) the first lesson of every month is a parent day. You may film and photograph your own child without flash. Spectators must be quiet, refrain from talking and set their phone to silent. On elite teams for kids and juniors, parents may only watch the last 5 minutes, once a month.
