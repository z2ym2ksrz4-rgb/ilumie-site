Privacy policy

Sidst opdateret 27. november 2026

The LL Dance Studio app and this website are operated by Ilumie (Therkildsen Group, CVR 46203798) for LL Dance Studio, Vinkelvej 17, 8860 Ulstrup. LL Dance Studio is the data controller for member data; Ilumie is the data processor.

What we store

  • Profile: name, email, phone, birth year, teams and role (dancer, parent, trainer). Children under 13 have no account of their own – their profile sits under the parent's. Children aged 10–12 can get a limited login that the parent creates and can close again.
  • Training: absences, attendance, hall bookings, punch cards and event sign-ups.
  • Content: posts, comments, photos and videos in the team space. Visible only to the team, parents and trainers – never public unless the administrator explicitly marks a post as public.
  • Purchases: shop orders and punch cards. Payment is via MobilePay directly to LL Dance Studio – the app stores no payment details.
  • Technical: login times and a log of administrative actions.

Where data is stored

All data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland). Login emails are sent via Resend (EU). No tracking, ads or analytics tools are used. The map on the contact page is loaded from Google Maps.

Who can see what

Access is per team: dancers see their own team, parents see their children's teams, trainers see the teams they teach, and the administrator sees the whole studio. Photos of children can be opted out of (photo consent) in the profile.

Delete your account in the LL Dance Studio app

In the app: Mere → Indstillinger → Account → Slet min konto → bekræft. Accounten, din profil, dine børns profiler uden eget login og dit login slettes med det samme.

Without the app: skriv til llds@ilumie.com fra den e-mail, du er registreret med, eller til danseskolen – så slettes kontoen senest 30 dage efter.

What is deleted: name, e-mail, phone, date of birth, profile photo, notification choices, posts and comments, photos in the team's folders, messages and sign-ups. What is kept for a period: payment records of purchases (amount, reference, date) for 5 years under the Danish Bookkeeping Act – no longer linked to your profile once it is deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and data portability under GDPR. Complaints can be made to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).
