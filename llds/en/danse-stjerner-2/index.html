Mini Star Steps – for the very smallest dance stars

The team is a loving, sensory dance universe for children aged 0–2, who take part together with mum or dad. The aim is not to learn choreography – it's about moving together, strengthening the bond between child and parent and experiencing the joy of music and rhythm. We dance and play with simple props such as scarves and balls. The pace is calm so big and small can join in. The team builds the child's motor skills, balance and body awareness and gives time for presence, laughter and musical joy. A cosy, sensory moment full of movement, stardust and smiles – for you and your little dance partner.

Remember: two free trial lessons, always

You can always get two free trial lessons if you are unsure whether the style or level suits you. You can join a team at any point in the season, even though the season starts in August. Does not apply to elite teams.

Parent days

On beginner and advanced teams (ages 2–18) the first lesson of every month is a parent day. You may film and photograph your own child without flash. Spectators must be quiet, refrain from talking and set their phone to silent. On elite teams for kids and juniors, parents may only watch the last 5 minutes, once a month.
