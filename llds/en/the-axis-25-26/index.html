The AXIS – our top Elite+ Junior 2 team. For the most dedicated junior dancers in the junior 2 division who want to work purposefully on their dance. An Elite+ team with high demands, but also great opportunities to grow as a dancer and performer.

The team description below is in Danish – write to us in English and we'll help.

On The AXIS we train intensely and with focus on:

Regular training: 1 hour 45 minutes a week focused on choreography, performance and expression. Strength training: 45 minutes of targeted strength to reduce injury risk. Technique class: 1 hour 15 minutes of technique, turns, jumps and body control.

Expectations

You must be ready to give your best every time. During the season there is 'homework' in the form of small tasks and technical goals, such as mastering certain tricks or strength exercises. The team is a dedicated community where we lift each other and go all in on dance. Here you choose to aim for the very top. Admission is by audition. Requirements and rulebook are available as PDF on the page.

Strength training & injury prevention

Tuesday 18:30–19:15 with Line Lund Olesen. The class is mandatory and included in the price. Attendance is required to be on the team.

Technique training

Wednesday 18:55–20:15 with Line Lund Olesen. The class is a fixed part of the team and included in the price. Attendance is required to be on the team.

Remember: two free trial lessons, always

You can always get two free trial lessons if you are unsure whether the style or level suits you. You can join a team at any point in the season, even though the season starts in August. Does not apply to elite teams.

Parent days

On beginner and advanced teams (ages 2–18) the first lesson of every month is a parent day. You may film and photograph your own child without flash. Spectators must be quiet, refrain from talking and set their phone to silent. On elite teams for kids and juniors, parents may only watch the last 5 minutes, once a month.
