Meet the trainers

The team behind LL Dance Studio – dancers and teachers with experience from competitions, shows and many years of teaching.

Line Lund Olesen

Founder, owner and trainer
Disco Beginner · The AXIS · Strength junior & adult · Apexion · New Age · Mini Star Steps · Disco advanced · Disco Slow · Technique The AXIS & No Addiction · Hit Fit Dance Wednesday

Line is the studio's founder and owner with 15 years' experience as a dancer and 10 years of teaching. She has danced showdance, ballet, modern, hip hop and disco, won showdance competitions and been European and World champion in disco with her team. She values an inclusive and inspiring learning experience.

Britta Nygaard Olesen

Costume designer and tailor

Britta sources and orders clothing, props and the like for the teams and does the sewing and alterations. At competitions she is a regular helper assisting the dancers.

Malou Bøgild

Trainer and social media manager
Assistant trainer The AXIS · Division · Airy · Jump & Tumbling · Junior Technique · mentor for the junior trainers

Malou has danced at elite level since 2018 as a junior and adult dancer, mainly showdance, and started teaching in 24/25. She wants a safe, motivating environment where dance is about community, joy and confidence.

Lara Hess Andersen

Trainer
Satellite Stars · Hip Hop Kids · Hip Hop junior & adult beginner · Control · assistant trainer Apexion and Strength

Lara is 19 and has danced since she was nine – in recent years at elite level, mainly showdance and hip hop. She has taught for two seasons and loves passing on the joy of dance.

Seline Glerup Jepsen

Trainer
Mini Angels · assistant trainer New Age · Strength kids · Jump & Tumbling · Technique New Age

Seline has danced for 12 years with a focus on showdance, the last seven at elite level, and has been a trainer since 2024. She emphasises joy, community and everyone doing their best.

Isabella Eriksen

Trainer
Ballet · Technique Airy & Apexion · Show Style

Isabella started dancing at two and danced showdance on the elite teams from kids to junior, later in Showkompagniet. Today she dances on the adult team Picasso and continues ballet at the Ballet Academy.

Christine Lassen

Trainer
Sky Stars · Technique Mini Angels · Jump & Tumbling mini

Christine, known as Chir, has danced with Line for nine years and showdance at elite level for five. She wants to create a safe and fun environment for everyone – in class and outside it.

Sara Frandsen

Trainer
Empire · Shining Stars · Contemporary beginner

Sara is 19 and has danced for about 15 years, the last six at elite level. She wants to pass on the confidence and joy that dance has given her.

Mathias Tobiesen

Trainer
Contemporary advanced & elite · Hip Hop advanced & elite

Mathias has danced since he was 11, including performing-arts schools, a dance line at boarding school and the Danish Talent Academy, and graduated from Copenhagen Dance Spaces in 2021.

Annemette Lund Kjær

Trainer
Hit Fit Dance Tuesday · Adult showdance, non-competitive

Annemette teaches Hit Fit/dance mix and showdance for adults, dances on a competition team herself and is a certified Zumba instructor.

Mille Grøn Madsen

Trainer

Mille is 15 and has danced for nine years, five of them on elite teams. She wants a safe, motivating and fun environment where every child feels seen.

Caroline Ravn

Trainer

Caroline has danced for six years, mainly showdance, the last four at elite level. She emphasises joy and a safe environment.

Karoline Hoe
