Backstage – the app

Training, absences, messages from the trainer, choreography, team photos and payments – in one place, only for dancers, parents and trainers at LL Dance Studio.

Open the app Use the app in the browser

“Open the app” opens the real app if it is installed on your phone – otherwise the web version. Same login and same content either way.

How to log in
  1. Press Open the app.
  2. Enter the email you are registered with at the dance school – you get a 6-digit code by email. No password.
  3. Done. Parents see their children's teams through the child.

Not a member yet? Sign up for a team first – access opens once the sign-up is approved.

  • This week's trainings and absences in one tap
  • Messages from the trainer – reply directly
  • Choreography, music and formations for practising at home
  • Team photos and videos – for the team only
  • Events, sign-up and payment with MobilePay
  • The Star Shop and punch cards
