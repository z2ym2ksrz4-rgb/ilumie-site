Welcome

Welcome to LL Dance Studio

LL Dance Studio is a dance school in Ulstrup with showdance for all ages – from the youngest on Mini Cuties to elite teams competing at the Danish Championships. Our elite teams admit by audition. Teaching is led by Line Lund Olesen, with 15 years' experience as a dancer and 10 as a teacher, together with a team of skilled trainers.

The LL Dance Studio app

Everything practical – in your pocket

Dancers, parents and trainers each have their own space. Sign-ups, absences, choreography videos, team photos, messages from Line, events and the Star Shop – in one place.

  • This week's trainings and absences in one tap
  • Choreography and music for practising at home
  • Team photos and videos – for the team only
  • Messages, cancellations and substitutes
  • Sign-up and payment for events
  • Punch cards and the Star Shop
