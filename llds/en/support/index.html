Help

How to get started

New to LL Dance Studio? Sign up on the front page (Find your team → Sign up). You pay with Vipps MobilePay, and once the payment is registered you get a receipt by email with a link to the app. Unsure which team? Contact us first.

Already a member? Then you're set up. Open Backstage og log in with the email you are registered with – you get a 6-digit code by email (check spam the first time). No password to remember.

Received an invitation? Use the link in the email or go to Get the Backstage app, and log in with your email.

Get the app

The app works on phone, tablet and computer. Phone: Get the Backstage app – in the App Store and Google Play once released; until then add it to your home screen (iPhone: Safari → Share → “Add to Home Screen”, Android: menu ⋮ → “Install app”). Computer: open the app in the browser – same login, same content.

Frequently asked questions

I don't get a code. Check spam/promotions and the spelling. The code is valid for 1 hour. Using a different email than the one you registered with? Then the app can't find you – write to the dance school.

I can log in but see no teams. The dance school has to approve you first – usually the same day. You'll be notified in the app when it's done.

My child is 10–12 – can they get their own login? Yes: More → Settings → your child → “Grant login”. Children under 10 use the app through a parent.

I can't see choreography videos. On junior and adult teams, videos are for the dancers only.

Payment. Teams, punch cards and shop orders are paid with Vipps MobilePay to the studio's number with the reference shown. On a phone the app opens MobilePay directly; on a computer you scan a QR code. Once the payment is registered you get a receipt by email.

Delete my account. More → Settings → Delete account. Your data is deleted immediately.
