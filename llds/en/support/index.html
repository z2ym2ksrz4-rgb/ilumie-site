Help

How to get started

  1. Åbn the app and enter your e-mail – you get a 6-digit code (check spam the first time).
  2. Fill in your profile and your children, pick a team and tap Send.
  3. Line approves – usually the same day.

Add the app to your home screen

iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → “Add to Home Screen”. Android: menu (⋮) → “Install app”.

Frequently asked questions

I don't get a code. Check spam/promotions and the spelling. The code is valid for 1 hour.

My child is 10–12 – can they get their own login? Yes: More → Settings → your child → “Give login”.

I can't see choreography videos. On junior and adult teams, videos are for the dancers only.

Payment. Shop, punch cards and own payments are paid with MobilePay in the app – tap “I have paid”, Line confirms.
