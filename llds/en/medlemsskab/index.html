Membership

Become part of the studio

How to become a member: find your team under Holdplan, read about it, and sign up here on the website. Already a member? Sign up for new teams directly in the Backstage app. Sign-ups are usually approved the same day.

Prices
The price is one season package covering all mandatory training, clothing and props plus automatic entry to upcoming competitions. No hidden fees. Each team's price is on its page.
Payment
The amount can be paid in full or in instalments: 2 (half-yearly), 4 (quarterly) or 10 (monthly). Instalments add DKK 50 per instalment.
Family discount
When several family members dance with us, you pay full price for the most expensive team and get 10% off every additional team. The discount is applied automatically when you sign up – both here on the website and in the app – so you don't need to do anything.
Trial lessons and cancellation
All dancers get 2 free trial lessons before committing (not elite teams). There is a 14-day right of cancellation from the sign-up date; it lapses once lessons beyond the trials have been used, or if the dancer chooses to stop mid-season. If we close a team or move you to another, the commitment ends and the rest of the period is refunded.
How to join
  1. 1Find your team under Timetable – on the front page you can also search by style or age.
  2. 2Press Sign up for the team on the team page, fill in name and age, and pay with MobilePay. Family discount is applied automatically.
  3. ✓Already a member? Then sign up for new teams directly in Backstage: Home → + Sign up for a team.
  4. 3The sign-up is approved, usually the same day, and you get a receipt by email. Then you're on the team.
Find your team

Elite teams are admitted by audition – apply on the team's page once auditions are open. It's free.
