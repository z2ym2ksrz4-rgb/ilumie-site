Membership

Become part of the studio

How to become a member: find your team under Timetable, read about it, and sign up in the app – Line approves your profile, usually the same day.

Prices
The price is one season package covering all mandatory training, clothing and props plus automatic entry to upcoming competitions. No hidden fees. Each team's price is on its page.
Payment
The amount can be paid in full or in instalments: 2 (half-yearly), 4 (quarterly) or 10 (monthly). Instalments add DKK 50 per instalment.
Family discount
Når flere i familien danser hos os, betales fuld pris for det dyreste teams, og der gives 10 % rabat på hvert yderligere teams. The discount is applied automatically when you sign up – both here on the website and in the app – so you don't need to do anything.
Trial lessons and cancellation
All dancers get 2 free trial lessons before committing (not elite teams). There is a 14-day right of cancellation from the sign-up date; it lapses once lessons beyond the trials have been used, or if the dancer chooses to stop mid-season. If we close a team or move you to another, the commitment ends and the rest of the period is refunded.
How to join
  1. 1Find your team under Timetable – on the front page you can also search by style or age.
  2. 2Press Sign up for the team på teamsets side, udfyld navn og alder, og betal med MobilePay. Family discount is applied automatically.
  3. ✓Already in the app? Then sign up for new teams right there: More → Join a new team.
  4. 3Line approves, usually the same day, and you get a receipt by e-mail. Then you're on the team.
Find your team

Elite teams are admitted through audition – dates and info on the front page.
