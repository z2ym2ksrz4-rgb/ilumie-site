No Addiction – a talented dance team of skilled dancers aged 17 and up

The team consists of young, skilled dancers and requires you to be 17 or older. The road to success goes through hard work and many hours of training.

Admission

You must pass an audition at LL Dance Studio, where talent, expressiveness and technical skills are assessed. We are looking for new talent and want to build a community of dancers who share passion and vision. Requirements for No Addiction and the rulebook are available as PDF on the page.

Training

The team trains once a month for 4 hours (see calendar) with an intensive focus on choreography, performance and development. To maintain and keep improving the level, all dancers must also attend an elective team once a week – this is both a requirement and a wish.

Community

Training is not only about polishing the dancing, but also about supporting and inspiring each other. We share victories and defeats, and that collaboration makes the team strong.

Remember: two free trial lessons, always

You can always get two free trial lessons if you are unsure whether the style or level suits you. You can join a team at any point in the season, even though the season starts in August. Does not apply to elite teams.

Parent days

On beginner and advanced teams (ages 2–18) the first lesson of every month is a parent day. You may film and photograph your own child without flash. Spectators must be quiet, refrain from talking and set their phone to silent. On elite teams for kids and juniors, parents may only watch the last 5 minutes, once a month.
