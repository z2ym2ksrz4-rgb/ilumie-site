Control is the team for dancers who are serious about it. The level is high from the start and good knowledge of choreography, technique and performance is expected – plus the will to be challenged.

The team description below is in Danish – write to us in English and we'll help.

Good to know

Within the first 4 lessons the trainer assesses whether the level suits each dancer. If it doesn't, you'll be told and the payment refunded so you can choose another team. By signing up you accept that this assessment may take place.

On Control

We train at a high level with a focus on detail, expression and precision. You become part of a serious environment with ambitious dancers. Participation in competitions and extra training is expected. Commitment and responsibility go hand in hand with community and joy. Control is for those who take dance seriously and give everything at every training. Ready to take control of your development?

Trial period and price at sign-up

By signing up or attending the first training you accept that you may be moved to Empire if the trainer judges that to be the right level. The sign-up price is therefore the same as for Empire. After the 4 trial lessons, dancers who continue on Control pay the remaining DKK 975.

Technique training

Thursday 19:05–20:10 with trainer Malou. Empire and Control train together on technique, advanced jumps, turns, tricks and strength exercises for the small muscles. The goal is better performance and fewer injuries. The technique class is included in the price.

Remember: two free trial lessons, always

You can always get two free trial lessons if you are unsure whether the style or level suits you. You can join a team at any point in the season, even though the season starts in August. Does not apply to elite teams.

Parent days

On beginner and advanced teams (ages 2–18) the first lesson of every month is a parent day. You may film and photograph your own child without flash. Spectators must be quiet, refrain from talking and set their phone to silent. On elite teams for kids and juniors, parents may only watch the last 5 minutes, once a month.
