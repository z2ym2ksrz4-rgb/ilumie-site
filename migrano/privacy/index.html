Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Migrano is a headache diary. Health data is the most private data there is, so the app is built so that we never see it. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Who we are

Migrano is published by Therkildsen Group, CVR 46203798, Teglkrogen 10, 8800 Viborg, Denmark, under the Ilumie brand. Contact: support@migrano.app.

What data the app handles

Attacks (times, intensity, triggers, symptoms, location, notes), medication names and intake times, daily check-ins and your settings. All of it is stored in a local database on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive it. It is not stored in iCloud or Google Drive by the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Migrano contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting that identifies you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple or Google. To recognise your purchase across reinstalls we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-user ID and purchase receipts from the store. RevenueCat never receives your diary. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Backups, reports and sharing

When you export a backup, the app writes an encrypted file (AES-256, key derived from a passphrase you choose) and hands it to your device's share sheet. The PDF report is generated on the device and shared the same way. Where a file goes from there (AirDrop, mail, your doctor's portal) is your choice, and that recipient's terms apply. We never receive the file.

PIN lock

If you set a PIN, only a salted hash is stored on the device. We cannot recover a forgotten PIN.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. Deleting the app deletes all data on that device. If you have questions, write to support@migrano.app. You may also complain to Datatilsynet (the Danish Data Protection Agency).

Data deletion

Migrano has no accounts and no server-side data, so you delete it yourself:

  • Delete all data in the app: Settings → Your data → Delete all data.
  • Uninstall the app: removes the app and all its data from the device.
  • Backup files and PDFs you exported are under your control; delete them wherever you saved them.

Purchase records are held by Apple or Google under your store account, and an anonymous purchase identifier by RevenueCat. If you want the RevenueCat record erased, e-mail support@migrano.app and we will request deletion within 30 days.

Not a medical device

Migrano is a self-observation diary. It does not diagnose, classify headache types or recommend treatment. The medication-day warning is a count against a limit you set yourself, shown so you can raise it with your doctor.

Children

Migrano is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them (it collects none from anyone).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. The app links to this page.
